Please try the controls, how easy the app is to use, and how well it works with accessibility features. For example:
- Search for stations, play them, and add a few to Favorites.
- Pause, stop, and skip between stations, from the app, the Lock Screen, headphones, or the Mac's keyboard.
- Try the sleep timer.
