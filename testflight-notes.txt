New in this build:
- Search results have an Add button (a star and the word "Add") to save a station to Favorites, and the Now Playing screen has Add to Favorites. No need to know about swiping or touching and holding.
- The keyboard goes away when a search starts, so the results show right away.
- Clear buttons beside the text fields.
- If a station won't connect, the Lock Screen now says so ("Connecting…" or "Not playing") instead of showing it as playing.
- With the screen off, one press of your AirPods or EarPods on a station that won't connect moves on to the next station. (With the screen off and nothing playing, iOS sends double and triple presses as a single press, so Next and Previous can't get through.)

Please try the controls, how easy the app is to use, and how well it works with accessibility features. For example:
- Search for stations, play them, and add a few to Favorites.
- Pause, stop, and skip between stations, from the app, the Lock Screen, headphones, or the Mac's keyboard.
- Try the sleep timer.
- If you use VoiceOver, Voice Control, Switch Control, Full Keyboard Access, Larger Text or other accessibility settings, please try the app with them, and tell me anything that's confusing, hard to reach, or not announced.
Feedback of any kind is welcome: use the TestFlight app's Send Feedback, or email scott_howell@icloud.com.
