# Crystal Set Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 14, 2026*

Crystal Set is an internet radio app for the Mac and iPhone, made by Scott Howell. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

Crystal Set doesn't collect any information about you. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your searches, your favorites or anything about how you use the app.

## What stays on your device

- **Your favorites list** is saved on your device.
- **A few app settings,** such as whether your favorites have been synced with iCloud yet, are saved on your device.

## What the app sends, and to whom

Crystal Set connects to these services only to do what you ask:

- **Radio Browser** (radio-browser.info), the free public directory of radio stations, when you search for stations. It receives your search words. The app also asks it for details (logo and country) of stations in your favorites, by their directory ID or stream address. When you play a station found through Radio Browser, the app tells Radio Browser which station was played, which helps rank popular stations. No information about you is included. Radio Browser's own privacy practices apply to what it receives.
- **Each radio station's server,** when you play that station. Like any website you visit, the station's server sees your device's internet (IP) address while it sends you the audio.
- **Station logos,** for stations that have one. The logo is fetched once from the address the station gives (usually its own website), which sees your device's internet (IP) address, like visiting the site would. Logos are then kept on your device.
- **Your own iCloud account,** if you're signed in to iCloud, to keep your favorites in step between your devices. This uses Apple's iCloud storage, which only you can access. The developer can't see it. Apple's privacy policy applies to iCloud.

Nothing else is sent anywhere.

## Files you import or export

When you import or export favorites, Crystal Set reads or writes only the file you choose. It doesn't keep a copy anywhere else.

## Children

Crystal Set collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
